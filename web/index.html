Comparing CO2 Levels Across Time

CO2 levels have changed a lot throughout Earth's history. This app looks at how atmospheric CO2 concentrations have changed over time, using modern measurements and historical climate records.

The data come from NOAA and historical ice core records used in Module 1.

Explore CO2 Levels

Choose a dataset and time period to see how CO2 levels have changed. You can also compare the different records to see how recent measurements relate to the past.

About the Data

Modern CO2 measurements come from the Mauna Loa Observatory in Hawaii. These measurements provide a record of atmospheric CO2 concentrations beginning in 1958.

Historical CO2 estimates come from air trapped in Antarctic ice cores. These records help scientists understand how atmospheric CO2 concentrations changed long before modern instruments existed.

Comparing the two datasets helps show how recent CO2 levels relate to changes over much longer periods of Earth's history.
